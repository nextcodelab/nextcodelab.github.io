Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how NextCodeLab handles information in connection with its applications, websites, and related services.

Effective: August 17, 2026

1. Introduction

NextCodeLab ("NextCodeLab", "we", "us", or "our") develops and publishes software applications and related services for platforms including Windows, Android, iOS, and other supported platforms.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you use our applications, websites, and services, how that information may be used, and the choices available to you.

Different NextCodeLab applications may have different features and data requirements. Where an individual application has additional privacy information, that information may supplement this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

We design our applications to collect only the information reasonably necessary to provide, maintain, secure, and improve the applicable service.

Depending on the application and features you use, information may include:

  • Information that you voluntarily provide, such as information submitted through a contact or support form.
  • Application settings and preferences stored locally on your device.
  • Information required to provide specific application functionality.
  • Technical information necessary for troubleshooting, security, diagnostics, and application reliability.
  • Information collected by third-party services integrated into a particular application, where applicable.

Many NextCodeLab applications can be used without creating an account or directly providing personal information.

3. Device and Diagnostic Information

Some applications may collect limited technical or diagnostic information when necessary to identify crashes, errors, performance problems, compatibility issues, or security problems.

Depending on the platform and services used by the application, diagnostic information may include information such as:

  • Device or computer type
  • Operating system and version
  • Application version
  • Crash information
  • Error information
  • Configuration information
  • Approximate usage or diagnostic information
  • IP address or similar technical information where provided by a third-party service

The exact information collected depends on the application, platform, configuration, and third-party services used by that application.

4. Information Stored on Your Device

Some NextCodeLab applications store information locally on your device so that the application can remember your settings, preferences, documents, progress, bookmarks, or other application data.

Information stored locally may remain on your device until you delete it, clear the application's data, uninstall the application, or otherwise remove the relevant files.

Local storage does not necessarily mean that the information is transmitted to NextCodeLab.

5. Third-Party Services

Some NextCodeLab applications may use third-party services to provide functionality such as application distribution, advertising, analytics, diagnostics, crash reporting, authentication, cloud services, notifications, or other technical services.

These third-party providers may process technical or other information according to their own privacy policies.

Third-party services are used only where applicable to the particular application.

Examples of third-party services

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google information about partner services
  • Microsoft Privacy Statement
  • Apple Privacy Policy

6. Advertising

Some NextCodeLab applications may display advertisements. Applications that contain advertising may use advertising providers that collect or process information in accordance with their own privacy policies.

Not all NextCodeLab applications contain advertisements.

Where applicable, advertising providers may use information such as device information, advertising identifiers, approximate location, application interaction information, or other technical information to provide and measure advertisements.

7. Analytics and Usage Information

Some applications may use analytics services to understand application usage and improve reliability, functionality, and user experience.

Analytics information may include events, application version, device type, operating system, and other technical information.

Analytics services are used only where implemented in the applicable application.

8. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our websites may use cookies, local storage, or similar technologies where necessary to remember preferences, provide functionality, understand website usage, or support third-party services.

Mobile and desktop applications generally do not use browser cookies in the same way as websites. However, an application may contain third-party components or web content that use similar technologies.

You can control cookies through your web browser's settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect some website functionality.

9. How We Use Information

Information may be used for purposes including:

  • Providing and operating our applications and services.
  • Maintaining application functionality.
  • Saving application preferences and settings.
  • Diagnosing crashes and technical problems.
  • Improving application performance and reliability.
  • Providing customer support.
  • Protecting against abuse, fraud, and security threats.
  • Understanding general application or website usage where analytics are enabled.
  • Displaying and measuring advertising where applicable.

10. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information.

Information may be processed by service providers that help us operate, maintain, secure, analyze, distribute, or support our applications and services.

We may also disclose information where required to comply with applicable law, legal process, governmental requests, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of NextCodeLab, our users, or others.

11. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, to provide the applicable service, to meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, or protect our legitimate interests.

Information stored locally by an application is generally controlled by the user and may be removed by deleting the relevant application data or uninstalling the application, subject to the operating system and application behavior.

12. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed through our applications and services.

However, no method of electronic transmission, internet communication, or electronic storage is completely secure. Therefore, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

13. Children's Privacy

Our services are not generally directed to children under the age of 13 unless an application specifically states otherwise.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without appropriate authorization where required by applicable law.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us inappropriately, please contact us so that we can investigate and take appropriate action.
